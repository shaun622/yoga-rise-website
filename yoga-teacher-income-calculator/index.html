How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in your numbers

    Complete the fields as accurately as you can. If you don’t know an exact figure, use your best estimate.

  2. Check your results

    Your results automatically update in Your Business at a Glance as you enter your numbers.

  3. Take a step back

    The numbers aren’t a measure of your worth or success. They’re information to help you understand your current business model.

  4. Choose your next action

    Review the levers to close the gap and choose one or two areas you’d like to explore or improve.

There isn’t one “right” way to build a yoga career. The goal is to understand your numbers so you can make decisions that are right for you, your business and the way you want to work.

What’s included

The income gap compares income after your chosen reserve with your annual take-home goal. The reserve is a simple percentage of positive profit, not a calculation of your actual tax.

The studio hourly rate is studio pay less the per-class cost, divided by your total time per class. Weekly hours include studio and corporate classes plus 75 minutes per private session, as assumed in this model. Workshop, retreat and other work hours are not included. Per-class expenses apply to studio and corporate classes; include other costs in annual expenses.
