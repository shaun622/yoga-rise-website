FAQ

Plan your day.

Who is YogaRise Expo for?

YogaRise Expo is designed for yoga teachers, studio owners, educators, teacher trainers and yoga professionals at every stage of their journey, from newly qualified to highly experienced.

Do I need to be a qualified yoga teacher?

No. If you're currently training, exploring a career in yoga, or work within the wider yoga industry, you're welcome to join us.

Can studio owners attend?

Absolutely. The Expo is an opportunity for studio owners to learn, connect with teachers and industry leaders, discover new tools and services, and build valuable relationships.

What's included in my ticket?

Our Expo Pass includes access to the Expo and vendor area, networking area and one session.

Our Full Day Pass includes the complete program, all sessions and workshops, Expo and networking areas, lunch, session replays, gift bag and eligible CPD points.*

Is lunch included?

Lunch is included with the Full Day Pass. It is not included with the Expo Pass.

Can I earn CPD points?

We're currently working towards CPD recognition for the Full Day experience. We'll share full details once confirmed.

Will sessions be recorded?

Yes, selected sessions will be recorded. Replays are included with the Full Day Pass, with optional replay access planned for Expo Pass holders.

Can I attend individual sessions?

The Expo Pass includes access to one session, subject to availability. If you'd like to experience the full program, the Full Day Pass gives you access to all sessions and workshops.

What's the refund/transfer policy?

Tickets can be transferred to another attendee if you're unable to attend. Our full cancellation and refund policy will be provided at the time of purchase.

How can my brand become a partner or exhibitor?

We partner with brands and organisations that align with the YogaRise community and want to meaningfully contribute to the industry.

From exhibiting and brand experiences to education and strategic partnerships, there are a number of ways to get involved.

Visit our Partner page to explore partnership and exhibitor opportunities.

Can I become a YogaRise Brand Ambassador?

Yes. We’re looking for yoga teachers, creators and community voices who believe in the YogaRise mission and want to help us spread the word.

As a Brand Ambassador, you’ll help introduce YogaRise to your community.

Interested? Visit our Brand Ambassador page to learn more.
