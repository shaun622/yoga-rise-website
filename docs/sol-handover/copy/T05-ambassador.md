# BECOME A YOGARISE AMBASSADOR

## Help us move yoga forward.

YogaRise is building a more connected, supported and sustainable future for yoga professionals and we want the community to be part of it.

We're looking for yoga teachers, creators and community voices who believe in what we're building and want to help us spread the word.

\[ BECOME AN AMBASSADOR \]

## WHAT IS A YOGARISE AMBASSADOR?

YogaRise Ambassadors are people who genuinely connect with our mission and want to introduce YogaRise to their own communities.

That might mean sharing the Expo, starting conversations, creating content or simply helping more yoga professionals discover what we're building.

You don't need a huge following.

We're more interested in alignment, authenticity and genuine connection with your community.

\[ BECOME AN AMBASSADOR \]

## WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

You might be a:

* Yoga Teacher  
* Studio Owner  
* Teacher Trainer  
* Content Creator  
* Wellness Professional  
* Community Leader  
* Wellbeing Enthusiast 

What matters most is that you believe in supporting the growth of yoga professionals and are excited to share YogaRise authentically.

\[ BECOME AN AMBASSADOR \]

## THE IMPORTANT BIT

We don't want you to become an ad.

We're not looking for scripted promotion or endless posts.

We want ambassadors who genuinely believe in what YogaRise is building and want to share it in their own voice.

Authenticity over promotion. Community over reach.

\[ BECOME AN AMBASSADOR \]

Your voice can help someone else find theirs.

That's how communities grow, one connection at a time.
