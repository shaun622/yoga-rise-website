## Your professional community, beyond the Expo.

**A year-round space to learn, connect, access opportunity and help shape the future of yoga.**

Whether you're newly qualified, building your teaching career, running a studio or looking for your next opportunity, YogaRise Membership gives you ongoing access to education, community and resources designed for the realities of being a yoga professional.

Continue building your skills, knowledge and confidence with ongoing education designed for the realities of a career in yoga.

MONTHLY MASTERCLASSES

TRAINING LIBRARY  
Access the growing library of YogaRise training and educational content.

MONTHLY ONLINE NETWORKING  
Connect with other yoga teachers and professionals every month.

INDUSTRY CONNECTIONS  
Create relationships that can lead to collaboration, learning and new opportunities.

Membership gives you more ways to participate in the YogaRise ecosystem.

EVENT DISCOUNTS  
Receive member pricing on YogaRise events and experiences.

PARTNER PERKS
