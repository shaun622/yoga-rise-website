# VOLUNTEER WITH YOGARISE



**Be part of what brings it all together.**

YogaRise is built around community, and our volunteers play an important role in bringing that community to life.

We're looking for friendly, reliable people who want to contribute to YogaRise Expo, connect with the industry and experience the event from behind the scenes.

\[ APPLY TO VOLUNTEER \]

## BE PART OF THE EXPERIENCE

**More than helping out.**

Volunteer your time, meet people from across the yoga industry and experience YogaRise from a different perspective.

**CONNECT**  
Meet yoga teachers, studio owners, educators, brands and the YogaRise community.

**EXPERIENCE**  
Enjoy access to selected parts of the Expo around your volunteer shift.\*

**CONTRIBUTE**  
Help us create a welcoming, connected experience for everyone attending.

**GO BEHIND THE SCENES**  
Get practical experience bringing a professional industry event to life.

\[ APPLY TO VOLUNTEER \]

## HOW YOU COULD HELP

**There's a role for different strengths.**

**WELCOME & REGISTRATION**  
Help attendees check in and find their way.

**SESSIONS & SPEAKERS**  
Support session logistics, speakers and attendee movement throughout the day.

**EXPO & PARTNERS**  
Help our exhibitors and partners throughout the event.

**EVENT OPERATIONS**  
Support the YogaRise team with the many moving pieces behind the scenes.

\[ APPLY TO VOLUNTEER \]

## WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

**Good people make great events.**

You don't need event experience or years of yoga training.

We're looking for people who are:

**Reliable · Friendly · Proactive · Calm under pressure · Comfortable with people · Happy to get involved**

An interest in yoga, wellness, events or community is a bonus.

\[ APPLY TO VOLUNTEER \]

## WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE

Depending on your role and shift, volunteers will receive access to selected parts of the Expo, food and refreshments during their shift, a YogaRise volunteer pack and opportunities to connect with the wider community.\*

\*Final volunteer benefits and access will depend on role and shift.

## HOW IT WORKS

01 → APPLY  
Tell us about yourself, your availability and how you'd like to contribute.

02 → GET MATCHED  
We'll match selected volunteers with roles based on strengths, interests and event requirements.

03 → GET BRIEFED  
We'll give you everything you need to feel prepared for the day.

04 → JOIN US  
Come help us bring YogaRise to life.
