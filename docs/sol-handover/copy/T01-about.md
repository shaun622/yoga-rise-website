## ABOUT YOGARISE

Raising the standard. Growing the industry.

**\> Building the future of the yoga industry.**

YogaRise is a platform created to support the people shaping the future of yoga.

Through education, connection and opportunity, we bring together teachers, studio owners, educators, industry leaders and aligned brands to help build a stronger, more connected and sustainable yoga industry.

## Why YogaRise Exists

Teacher training is the beginning. What comes next matters too.

Yoga teachers invest deeply in learning their craft. But once the initial training is complete, navigating what comes next can feel far less clear.

How do you continue developing as a teacher?  
Build a sustainable career?  
Connect with the right people?  
Understand the business behind your work?

YogaRise was created to help bridge that gap.

YogaRise exists to bridge that gap, bringing together the knowledge, people and resources that help yoga professionals continue to learn, connect and grow.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to support a stronger, more connected and sustainable yoga industry through education, community and opportunity.

**ELEVATE**

## OUR VISION

**A stronger, more sustainable future for yoga.**

Yoga teachers make a meaningful difference in people's lives. We believe they deserve careers that support their own lives, too.

Our vision is an Australian yoga industry where teachers are valued for their work, fairly compensated, supported in their growth, and able to build sustainable careers without burning themselves out in the process.

An industry where learning continues beyond certification, knowledge is shared, meaningful work is recognised, and connection creates opportunity.

**When teachers thrive, the industry rises with them.**

## FROM OUR FOUNDER

**Built from both sides of the industry.**

I trained as a yoga teacher in India and have since taught thousands of hours across studios, corporate environments, retreats and community events, while building one of Sydney's largest AcroYoga communities. 

Alongside yoga, my career has spanned marketing, business growth, leadership and coaching.

When I started building my own yoga business, I realised how fortunate I was to already understand marketing and business. I knew how to find clients, communicate my value and create opportunities around the work I loved.

But I also saw incredibly talented teachers struggling to make their work financially sustainable, often teaching more, earning less and eventually burning out.

**That never sat right with me.**

Yoga teachers do meaningful work. I believe they deserve the knowledge, support, connections and opportunities to build careers that are meaningful and sustainable.

That's a big part of why YogaRise exists.

**I believe we have an opportunity to build a stronger industry, together.**

Valerie Saindon  
Founder, YogaRise
