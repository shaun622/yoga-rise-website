Your marketing should help people understand:

**WHO YOU ARE**  
What makes your approach or expertise relevant?

**WHAT YOU OFFER**

**WHO YOU'RE FOR**  
Are you speaking to the people you actually want to attract?

**WHY THEY SHOULD TRUST YOU**  
Are you demonstrating your experience, expertise and value?

**WHAT TO DO NEXT**  
Can interested people easily take the next step?
