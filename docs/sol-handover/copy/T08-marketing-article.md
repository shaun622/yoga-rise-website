# **Does Your Marketing Support the Business You’re Building?**

Marketing can easily become another thing on the to-do list: post more, create more, show up more.

But before you add more to your marketing, it can be useful to step back and look at what you already have.

Is your positioning clear? Are your offers easy to understand? Are you reaching the right people? And when someone is interested, is it easy for them to take the next step?

That's what the YogaRise Marketing Health Check is designed to help you explore.

## Being visible isn't the same as being clear.

Someone might discover your Instagram and still not understand:

What you actually offer.

Who you work with.

What makes your approach different.

Why they should choose you.

Or what they should do next.

And when those pieces aren't clear, posting more rarely solves the underlying problem.

## Think about your marketing as a journey.

Someone discovers you.

↓

They become interested.

↓

They start to understand what you do.

↓

They develop trust.

↓

They decide they want to work with you.

↓

They know how to take the next step.

Your marketing needs to support that journey.

Not every post needs to sell something. But your overall marketing should make that journey easier.

---

## Start with the foundations.

At YogaRise, we believe building a sustainable yoga career requires more than being a great teacher.

You also need to understand the business around your work.

And marketing is part of that.

That doesn't mean becoming an influencer.

It doesn't mean posting every day.

It doesn't mean turning yourself into a personal brand that doesn't feel like you.

It means being able to communicate your value clearly to the people you want to reach.

## So, how healthy is your marketing?

We've created the **YogaRise Marketing Health Check** to help you find out.

The scorecard looks at seven areas:

**Positioning** \- Can people understand what you do?

**Offer** \- Is it clear how they can work with you?

**Online presence** \- Does your digital presence reflect your professionalism?

**Content & visibility** \- Are you reaching the right people?

**Trust & connection** \- Are you building credibility and relationships?

**Conversion** \- Is it easy for interested people to take the next step?

**Strategy** \- Are you marketing intentionally?

You'll receive an overall score as well as a breakdown of each area.

Most importantly, you'll identify **where to focus next.**

---

## **You don't need to fix everything.**

This is probably the most important part.

If your positioning is unclear, creating more content isn't necessarily the answer.

If people understand your offer but don't know how to book, you may not need more followers.

If your marketing is strong but you're exhausted by constantly creating content, you might need a simpler system.

**The right next step depends on where you are.**

That's why we created the scorecard.

Not to give you another list of things you *should* be doing.

But to help you understand what might actually matter most.
