The YogaRise Awards are coming in 2027, recognising the teachers, leaders, studios and organisations making a meaningful contribution to the Australian yoga industry.

Why the YogaRise Awards?

Great work deserves to be recognised.

Every day, people across the yoga industry are creating impact, teaching, mentoring, building communities, growing studios, sharing knowledge and finding new ways to move the industry forward.

The YogaRise Awards will create a space to recognise those contributions and celebrate the people behind them.

Not simply for popularity or visibility, but for impact, contribution and the difference they're making.

Award Categories

What we'll be celebrating.

The final categories will be announced ahead of the inaugural YogaRise Awards in 2027.

For now, we're exploring categories including:

Yoga Teacher of the Year

Recognising excellence, dedication and impact through teaching.

Emerging Yoga Teacher

Celebrating a newer teacher making a meaningful early contribution to the industry.

Yoga Studio of the Year

Recognising a studio creating an exceptional experience for its teachers and community.

Community Impact Award

Celebrating an individual or organisation using yoga to create meaningful positive impact.

Yoga Educator of the Year

Recognising those contributing to the continued education and development of yoga teachers.

Industry Partner of the Year

Recognising a business or organisation actively supporting the growth and sustainability of yoga professionals.

Industry Innovation Award

Celebrating fresh ideas, initiatives or businesses helping move the yoga industry forward.

Lifetime Contribution Award

Honouring someone whose long-term contribution has helped shape yoga in Australia.
