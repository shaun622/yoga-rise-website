Why YogaRise exists

Your training was the beginning. Not the destination.

Completing your teacher training is one of the most significant things you’ll ever do. But it’s just the start. Most teachers finish certified, capable and completely on their own – no roadmap for what comes next, no community to lean on, no clear path from passion to a sustainable income.

That’s the gap YogaRise was built to close. We bring the Australian yoga industry together through world-class events, ongoing education and a connected professional community.

The only platform built for yoga teachers, not just yoga lovers.

✺

01. Built for the profession

Every other yoga event in Australia is built for people who practise yoga. YogaRise is built for the people who teach it. Practical education, industry-leading speakers and conversations about building a real career and a real business.

✥

02. Raising the standard

Yoga teaching is an ancient profession, and it deserves to be treated like one. We give teachers access to education, mentorship and industry recognition that reflect the profound nature of the work – not just the lifestyle around it.

⌘

03. Somewhere to grow, year-round

YogaRise isn’t a one-day event you forget about by Monday. From the annual Expo to Awards, Membership and community Events, everything we build is designed to keep teachers learning, connected and moving forward.
