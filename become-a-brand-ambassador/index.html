What is a YogaRise Ambassador?

YogaRise Ambassadors are people who genuinely connect with our mission and want to introduce YogaRise to their own communities.

That might mean sharing the Expo, starting conversations, creating content or simply helping more yoga professionals discover what we're building.

You don't need a huge following.

We're more interested in alignment, authenticity and genuine connection with your community.
