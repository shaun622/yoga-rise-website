Meet Our Team

The people behind YogaRise

YogaRise is built by teachers, marketers and industry insiders who know exactly what it takes to build a real career in yoga.

Valerie Saindon

Founder & CEO

With over 20 years teaching movement, alongside nearly a decade in yoga and marketing, Valerie founded YogaRise to bridge the gap between yoga teacher training and a sustainable career, bringing education, connection and professional opportunity together in one place.

Nic Dorsch

Community & Event Coordinator

Nic Dorsch is an experienced yoga teacher, mentor and movement educator with over 22 years of teaching experience. At YogaRise, she brings her industry knowledge and community connections to support event coordination and the relationships that bring the community together.

Emma Scott

Brand Growth Strategist

Emma is the brand strategist behind YogaRise, an educator specialising in business and brand growth for yoga businesses. She works with yoga teachers and studio owners to help them build brands that actually reflect the value of what they do, and brought that expertise to shaping YogaRise from the ground up.

Peter Solway

Partnership Director

Peter leads YogaRise’s sponsor and partner relationships. He works closely with aligned brands to understand their goals, explore meaningful ways to collaborate and create partnerships that deliver value for both the organisation and the YogaRise community.

Derek Abel

Storyteller & Media Producer

Derek supports YogaRise in capturing the people, ideas and moments that bring the community to life. Through video and visual storytelling, he helps extend the YogaRise conversation beyond the event and share its impact with a wider audience.
