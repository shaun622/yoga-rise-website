What we’re looking for

We’re interested in people who can bring depth, experience and practical value to the YogaRise community.

That could include expertise across yoga teaching and methodology, anatomy and movement, philosophy, retreats and experiences, studio ownership, business and marketing, branding, leadership, wellbeing, technology, professional standards, finance, legal and other areas relevant to building a sustainable career in yoga.

You don’t necessarily need to be a yoga teacher.

But you do need to understand who you’re speaking to and how your expertise can support them.

We particularly value presenters who are generous with their knowledge, grounded in real experience and interested in contributing to the industry, not simply promoting themselves.

Have something valuable to share?

We’re looking for collaborators, not just presenters.

We want to develop long-term relationships with educators, experts and industry voices who believe in what we’re building.

That could begin with an Expo presentation and evolve into masterclasses, workshops, content, community conversations or future programs.

Submitting an expression of interest doesn’t guarantee a presenting opportunity, but it helps us understand your expertise and consider where there may be genuine alignment across YogaRise events, education and community.
