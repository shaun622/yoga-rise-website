Where the industry comes together.

Teaching yoga can sometimes feel like working in your own little world.

Different studios. Different schedules. Different communities.

YogaRise Events create more opportunities for us to come together, to meet the people working alongside us, exchange ideas and build stronger relationships across the industry.

Some events will be about learning.

Some will be about connection.

And some will simply give us the space to have conversations we don’t often get to have.

What to expect

Networking events

Meet the people behind the practice.

Bringing together yoga teachers, studio owners, educators and industry professionals. Come to meet new people, reconnect with familiar faces, exchange ideas and become more connected to the wider yoga community.

Online training & masterclasses

Practical education, wherever you are.

Learn from experienced teachers, industry experts and specialists through accessible sessions covering the important topics. From teaching and movement to business, marketing, leadership and professional development.

In-person workshops

Go deeper. Learn together.

Practical, interactive workshops designed to give you the time and space to explore a subject in greater depth. Learn directly from experienced presenters, ask questions, practise new skills and connect with other professionals in the room.
